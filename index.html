🎲 The Soccer Randomness Factor — Even with all the data in the world, football is unpredictable. This simulator uses Poisson distribution for goal scoring (the most statistically accurate model for football), plus an 8% "giant killer" chance per match — recreating moments like Japan beating Germany (2022) or Morocco knocking out Spain (2022). Knockout matches use penalty shootout logic when scores are level, with only a slight edge to the stronger team. Upsets are not just possible — they're built in.
