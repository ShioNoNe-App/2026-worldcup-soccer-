What is Soccer Predictor 2026?

Soccer Predictor 2026 is a free, data-driven simulation tool that lets you predict the outcome of every match in the 2026 FIFA World Cup — from the Group Stage all the way to the Final.

Unlike simple bracket pickers, our simulator uses a 5-factor power score model that combines real-world data to calculate each team\'s true strength, then simulates matches using statistically accurate methods.

How It Works

🏅

FIFA Ranking (35%)

Official April 2026 ranking points — the strongest single predictor of match outcomes.

💰

Market Value (25%)

Total squad value from Transfermarkt. England €1.3B vs Haiti €12M — it matters.

🧱

Squad Depth (15%)

How evenly talent is distributed. A balanced squad is harder to break down.

🧠

Manager Rating (15%)

Coaching quality: experience, tactical rep, and recent results combined.

🔥

Recent Form (10%)

Win rate over the last 10 international matches heading into the tournament.

The Randomness Factor

Football is unpredictable — and we have built that in. The simulator uses Poisson distribution for goal scoring (the most statistically accurate model for football), with an 8% "giant killer" chance per match. This recreates moments like Japan beating Germany in 2022, or Morocco knocking out Spain. Penalty shootouts are almost coin-flips, with only a tiny edge to the stronger team.
